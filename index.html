01 / Why Continuum

One system.
Many moving parts.

Different work needs different specialists. Continuum brings their progress together, making handoffs and human oversight easier to understand.

01

Work in parallel

Separate workstreams help different kinds of processes advance without forcing everything into a single queue.

02

See what matters

A shared overview makes responsibilities, changes, reviews and unfinished work visible.

03

Keep people in control

Human decisions remain explicit. A proposed action is not the same thing as permission to execute it.

      03 / Development & evidence

      Real research.
      Clear boundaries.

      Continuum has an existing private macOS prototype with a local coordination index, specialist engine groups and human review controls under development. Those components are separate from this public-facing demonstration.

      What this website proves: a working, accessible browser-only workflow simulator, review decisions, progress tracking, export and reset. What it does not prove: a production cloud service, continuous remote AI execution, verified savings or certification.
