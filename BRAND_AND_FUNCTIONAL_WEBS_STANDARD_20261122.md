# Continuum — brand architecture and functional website requirements
Date: 2026-11-22. Internal Unfire standard.

## Brand
- The product is named **Continuum**, never "Unfire Continuum" in public headers, hero copy, menu labels, logos or product titles.
- **Unfire** is the parent organization, credited sparingly as "by Unfire" in the brand signature or footer and as the legal publishing entity where verified.
- Apply the same product-first naming rule to new apps and their websites unless there is a legal name/marketplace constraint. Do NOT mass-rename existing apps or change bundle IDs, domains, registrations, signatures or published store listings automatically.

## All product websites must be genuinely functional
1. A marketing landing page alone is not considered a functional product website.
2. Expose a meaningful, safe user interaction or real product workflow when possible, including data entry, state transitions, validation, outcome and recovery.
3. Distinguish clearly: live service vs browser demo vs scripted sample vs waitlist; never claim synthetic actions have affected real customers.
4. Do not expose confidential native repositories, internal software or metadata to satisfy a public demo requirement.
5. Before release: test in Safari and at a narrow mobile viewport; verify keyboard, accessibility labels, error states, privacy, security, responsive layout, performance, provenance, rights, linked content and zero-cost hosting.
6. No paid features, service calls or mandatory login without express approval. Cost 0 EUR, remote-only, email-first external contact.
7. Check the inventory and existing site first, and record the URL, test evidence, owner and deployment status. Do not replace or edit another session's files without coordination.
8. Require a real, functioning backend only if a product's advertised purpose needs it. Otherwise clearly label an offline/sample interaction and avoid calling it a live product.
